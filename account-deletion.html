CyberTome account and data deletion

CyberTome does not create a CyberTome user account. You sign in with Google on the TV. Billing uses your Amazon account. There is no CyberTome cloud copy of your calendar, tokens, or ZIP code, so there is nothing to delete on a CyberTome server. You can complete this without reinstalling the app.

Remove CyberTome data

  1. Uninstall CyberTome from the Fire TV. That deletes Google tokens, cached calendar data, and local settings (including ZIP) stored by the app.
  2. If the app is still installed and you only want to disconnect Google, open CyberTome Settings and sign out.
  3. Revoke access at Google Account → Third-party apps (remove CyberTome).
  4. To stop charges, cancel CyberTome in Amazon account → Your Memberships and Subscriptions. Amazon keeps its own purchase records.
